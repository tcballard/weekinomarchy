## October 2 — HackerOne bug bounty

DHH, on Omarchy News: the Omacom Foundation funded a HackerOne bug bounty with $100,000. Reports go through HackerOne or security@omarchy.org. Mehmet İnce is joining Omarchy Core as head of security.

## October 8 — SpaceXAI

DHH, on Omarchy News: SpaceXAI is a Founding Corporate Patron, donating $1.5 million in Grok tokens. The post names Grok 4.7, Omabot, about 7,000 pull requests, and more than 550 contributors. It puts total backing at approximately $23.2 million, alongside Meta Superintelligence Labs, DigitalOcean, and Alibaba Cloud.

## October 9 — Namespace

DHH, on Omarchy News: Namespace is a Distinguished Corporate Patron, contributing $100,000 a year in compute for three years for ARM builds and QA. The post contrasts a three-minute emulated ARM ISO boot with a four-second boot on Namespace’s Apple Silicon runners, lists the other Distinguished Corporate Patrons, and puts total backing at approximately $23.5 million.
