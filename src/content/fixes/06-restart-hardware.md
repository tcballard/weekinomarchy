The heading says to restart the subsystem on its own before rebooting. Update > Hardware in the Omarchy menu has Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Audio, and Trackpad. The chapter’s examples are a Bluetooth headset that will not reconnect, a trackpad that went dead after suspend, and sound that vanished when a monitor was unplugged.
