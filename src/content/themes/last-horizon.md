The [v4.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy/releases/tag/v4.0.0) say “Add Solitude, Last Horizon, and Lupine as new themes.” and “Add Last Horizon and Solitude themes by @HANCORE-linux”. This directory is `themes/last-horizon` on the quattro branch.

manual/06-themes.md does not caption this directory in its gallery.

That chapter says each theme styles the desktop, terminal, neovim, the btop activity screen, Chromium, and the Omarchy shell: top bar, menu, notifications, OSD, and the lock screen. It also says Obsidian needs the Omarchy theme selected in the app under Appearance > Themes.

The background, accent, and foreground values on this page are copied from `themes/last-horizon/colors.toml`. The preview is `themes/last-horizon/preview.png` from the same directory.
