manual/06-themes.md captions this entry “Retro 82”. It lives at `themes/retro-82` on the quattro branch.

That chapter says each theme styles the desktop, terminal, neovim, the btop activity screen, Chromium, and the Omarchy shell: top bar, menu, notifications, OSD, and the lock screen. It also says Obsidian needs the Omarchy theme selected in the app under Appearance > Themes.

The background, accent, and foreground values on this page are copied from `themes/retro-82/colors.toml`. The preview is `themes/retro-82/preview.png` from the same directory.
